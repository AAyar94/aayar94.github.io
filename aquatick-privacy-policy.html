1. Introduction

Welcome to Aquatick ("we," "our," or "us"). This privacy policy explains what information Aquatick handles when you use our Android application and its Wear OS companion (together, the "App"), what leaves your device, and the choices you have. By using Aquatick, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

In short: Aquatick has no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. You can use the whole App as a guest, and your drink log and profile then stay on your device. A Google sign-in is only needed for the optional cloud backup, a Premium feature that you can switch off, and delete together with your account, at any time.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data you enter in the App

When you set up Aquatick you can enter your name, gender, age, weight, activity level, daily water goal and wake-up and bedtime. You also log drinks (type, amount and time), create custom drinks and set reminder times. This data, together with your settings, is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is used to calculate your goal, streaks, history and statistics. It is not sent to us unless you turn on cloud backup (see 2.3).

2.2 Account (optional)

You can sign in with Google. Aquatick never sees or stores a password. Google gives the App a signed token with your email address, name and profile picture link; accounts are run by Supabase, which stores these details and handles sign-in. Like any online service, Supabase and Google process technical data such as your IP address when the App contacts them. The sign-in session is kept in the App's private storage and is not included in Android backups. Without signing in, the App works as a guest.

2.3 Cloud backup (optional)

If you own Aquatick Premium, are signed in and turn on Data backup and Synchronization in Settings, Aquatick copies your drink log to a Supabase database in Europe: for each drink, its type, amount in millilitres and the time, plus a random identifier. Rows are protected so that only the signed-in account that created them can read, change or delete them. Your name, age, weight, goal, photo and settings are not uploaded. If you use several devices with the same account, the log is kept in step between them, including drinks you delete. Turning the switch off stops uploads; your cloud copy stays until you delete your account.

2.4 Camera and profile photo

If you choose to take a profile photo, Aquatick asks for camera access. The photo is saved in the App's private storage on your device and is never uploaded. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings.

2.5 Notifications and alarms

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. Aquatick uses the notification permission, exact alarms and a boot receiver only so reminders arrive on time and are re-scheduled after a restart or an update. You can switch reminders off in Settings.

2.6 Wear OS companion

If you use the Aquatick watch app, your phone and watch exchange your daily total, goal and the drinks you log on the watch through Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer, directly between your own devices. Aquatick does not send this data to us.

2.7 Android backup

Your drink log and settings may be included in Android's own backup (for example Google backup to your Google account) and in device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by your Android backup settings and handled by Google; Aquatick does not receive it. The sign-in session is excluded.

2.8 Purchases (Premium)

Aquatick Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment; Aquatick only learns whether the purchase exists so it can unlock Premium, and remembers that on your device so Premium also works offline. Aquatick never sees or stores your card or payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core functionality — calculating your goal, logging drinks, showing history, statistics and streaks, and syncing with your watch
  • Reminders — scheduling and delivering local notifications
  • Account and backup — signing you in with Google and, with Premium and if you turn it on, backing up and syncing your drink log
  • Premium — confirming your purchase through Google Play
  • Compliance — fulfilling legal obligations where applicable

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

Aquatick does not sell, trade or rent your personal information, and it shows no ads. We use these third-party services for the limited purposes above:

  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) — sign-in with your Google account. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — account storage and the optional cloud backup. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play and Google Play services (Google LLC) — app distribution, purchases, the Wear OS data connection and Android backup. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

Your data on the device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Data sent to Supabase travels over encrypted connections (TLS), the App never handles a password, and access to cloud rows is restricted to the account that owns them. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device remains as long as the App is installed; you can delete it by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android Settings. Account and cloud backup data remain until you delete your account. To delete it, open Settings → Account → Delete Account in the App. This permanently removes your account and all drinks backed up to the cloud. Drinks stored on your device are kept until you remove them yourself. If you cannot use the App, email us and we will delete your account on request.

8. Children's Privacy

Aquatick is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You can use Aquatick without an account, switch cloud backup and reminders off in Settings, log out, delete your account, and revoke the camera and notification permissions in Android Settings. Depending on your jurisdiction you may also have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
