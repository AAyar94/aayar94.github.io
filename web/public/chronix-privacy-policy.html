1. Introduction

Welcome to Chronix ("we," "our," or "us"). This privacy policy explains what information Chronix handles when you use our Android application (the "App"), what leaves your device, and the choices you have. By using Chronix, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

In short: Chronix has no account, no ads and no advertising ID. Your countdowns stay on your device. The App can send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and you can switch both off in Settings → Privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data you create

Your countdowns (titles, dates, notes, categories, colors, reminders, repeat rules) and your preferences are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server.

2.2 Calendar data

If you choose Import from calendar, Chronix asks for read-only access to your calendar (READ_CALENDAR). Events are read on your device and shown to you; only the events you select are copied into Chronix as countdowns. Calendar data is not transmitted anywhere. You can hide this feature in Settings and revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings.

2.3 Notifications and alarms

Reminders and milestone notifications are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. Chronix uses the notification permission, exact alarms and a boot receiver only so reminders arrive on time and are re-scheduled after a restart.

2.4 Usage analytics (optional)

Chronix uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how features are used. It records anonymous events such as a countdown being created, edited, reached or deleted, together with its category, how many days away it is, and whether it has a reminder or repeats. Firebase also collects some technical information automatically, such as a random app-installation identifier, app version, device model, Android version and language, and approximate region.

Chronix does not send your countdown titles, notes or dates, your calendar content, your contacts, or your advertising ID. Ad personalization is disabled. Usage analytics is on by default and can be switched off in Settings → Privacy → Usage analytics; once off, no analytics data is collected.

2.5 Crash reports (optional)

Chronix uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to find and fix crashes. A crash report contains technical details such as the stack trace, the app version, device model, Android version, and the state of the device at the time of the crash, together with the app-installation identifier. Crash reports are on by default and can be switched off in Settings → Privacy → Crash reports.

2.6 Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment; Chronix only learns whether the purchase exists so it can unlock Premium. Chronix never sees or stores your card or payment details.

2.7 Backups and sharing you start

Export countdowns saves a file to a location you choose, and Import countdowns reads a file you choose. When you share a countdown as an image or text, it goes only to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. Chronix does not transmit these files or images itself.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core functionality — creating, displaying and managing countdowns, including home screen widgets, the Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
  • Calendar import — reading calendar events you choose to add as countdowns
  • Reminders — scheduling and delivering local notifications
  • Premium — confirming your purchase through Google Play
  • App improvement — analyzing anonymous usage data and crash reports to fix bugs and improve features (only while those switches are on)
  • Compliance — fulfilling legal obligations where applicable

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

Chronix does not sell, trade or rent your personal information, and it shows no ads. We use these third-party services for the limited purposes above:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) — anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, optional. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play (Google LLC) — purchases. See the Google Privacy Policy.

No calendar data or countdown content is shared with third parties.

6. Data Security

Your countdowns are stored locally on your device and protected by Android's app sandbox. We do not operate servers that store your countdown data. Data sent to Firebase and Google Play travels over encrypted connections (TLS). However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention and Device Backup

Your countdown data remains on your device as long as the App is installed. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android Settings. Chronix's database is excluded from Google cloud backup; when you set up a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, the data may be copied to it. Analytics and crash data held by Google is kept under Firebase's standard retention settings. Switching a privacy switch off stops new collection.

8. Children's Privacy

Chronix is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You can switch usage analytics and crash reports off at any time in Settings → Privacy, revoke the calendar and notification permissions in Android Settings, and delete all local data by clearing the App's data. Chronix has no accounts, so the data we could hold about you is limited to the anonymous analytics and crash data described above, which is tied to a random installation identifier rather than to your identity. Depending on your jurisdiction you may also have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
